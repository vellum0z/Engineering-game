# Curriculum map

This page is for learners and teachers. It lists which course topics each level teaches (handheld mode) and tests (manual mode). Everything is played in the build yard, a physics sandbox: you build it, run it, and the simulation decides. Section numbers follow the topic order of Hibbeler's *Engineering Mechanics: Statics* (14th ed.); the game is an independent companion and contains no material from the book.

## Act I: Rustline Depot (Chapter 1, General Principles)

| Level | Topics | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Still or Moving | Branches of mechanics (rigid-body, deformable-body, fluid); statics vs dynamics; equilibrium includes constant velocity; statics as the a = 0 case; history (Archimedes, Galileo, Newton) | 1.1 |
| H2 Push and Pull | Basic quantities: length, time, mass, force; a force's magnitude, direction and point of application; contact vs distance forces; time drops out of statics | 1.2 |
| H3 Good Enough Models | Idealizations: particle, rigid body, concentrated force, and when each is good enough | 1.2 |
| H4 Newton's Yard | Newton's three laws in a non-accelerating frame; F = ma (Eq. 1–1); action and reaction act on different bodies; momentum form | 1.2 |
| H5 The Long Fall | Newton's law of gravitation (Eq. 1–2); weight W = mg (Eq. 1–3); g from GM/r²; mass is absolute, weight is not | 1.2 |
| H6 Three Rulers | Base and derived units; SI (N = kg·m/s²) and U.S. Customary (slug = lb·s²/ft) systems; W = mg with 9.81 m/s² and 32.2 ft/s² (Eqs. 1–4, 1–5) | 1.3 |
| H7 Trade Ledger | Converting between FPS and SI with factors equal to one; mixed-unit quantities such as impulse, density, pressure and torque | 1.3 |
| H8 Stamp the Plate | SI prefixes; rules for SI form (one prefix, none in a denominator except kg, the 0.1–1000 range, exponents apply to the prefix); computing with prefixes | 1.4 |
| H9 Balance the Books | Dimensional homogeneity; checking algebra with units | 1.5 |
| H10 Measure Twice | Significant figures and engineering notation; the rounding rule for an exact 5; keeping precision through a calculation; measurement uncertainty (go deeper) | 1.5 |
| H11 The Engineer's Loop | The general procedure for analysis: read, tabulate, apply principles, solve, judge the answer | 1.6 |

### Manual projects

Each manual project is a job card and the full parts library, with no explanations. It tests the topics above and adds at least one real-world factor the textbook leaves out.

| Project | Tests | Real-world factor |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Ridge Station Winch | Weight, statics as a = 0, SI and FPS units, prefixes | Gravity at the site's latitude and altitude |
| P2 Caravan Manifest | FPS to SI conversions of weight, speed, density, pressure and volume, with the short ton | Every rating plate is the failure load, with no hidden margin |
| P3 Controller Labels | SI form and prefixes, prefix in a denominator, exponents on prefixes, significant figures | The kilogram-force is defined with standard gravity, not local gravity |
| P4 Calibration Bench | Prefix arithmetic, pressure from force and area, rounding an exact 5, keeping precision through a calculation | Caliper uncertainty decides how many figures an area can claim; real hole clearances |
| P5 Ghost Satellite & Moon Rig | Gravitation, weight at altitude, dimensional homogeneity of G, weight and mass in FPS on the Moon | Moon gravity from GM/R², orbital debris at 400 km |
| P6 Settlement Standard | Comparing a mass to a standard, weight in SI and FPS, F = ma for a lift, choosing a rated sling | The 2019 SI kilogram, a pendulum-measured local g and a corroded reference kilogram |
